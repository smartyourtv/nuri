# Ilona Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 19, 2026** (version 0.4.0 added Ilona's voice
and listening — see "Ilona's voice and listening" below; the change
is explained there in the same plain words as the rest of this page)

**A note on the name:** this app was called Nuri in versions before 0.3.3. It was renamed Ilona on October 18, 2026. It is the same app, from the same maker — only the name changed.


Ilona is a free app. It helps older adults use their phone, remember
their medicine, and get help when something is confusing.

This page explains, in plain words, what Ilona does with your
information. Please read it slowly. Nothing here is hidden.

**Who makes Ilona:** Ilona is made by Shawn Alan Kiper, an individual in
California, USA. It is free software. There is no company and no
advertising behind it.

**Questions about privacy:** email **smartyourtv@gmail.com**. (If this
address ever changes, the new address will be listed here and in the
app.)

---

## The short version

- Ilona keeps your information **on your phone**. There is no Ilona
  server. Your information is not sent to the person who made Ilona.
- Ilona has **no accounts**, **no advertising**, and **never sells
  information** — to anyone, ever.
- There is **one exception**, explained below: a Google tool inside
  the app (ML Kit) sends Google small technical reports about how
  well the tool itself is working. It does **not** send your photos,
  your words, or your personal information.
- One more thing can happen, **only if a caregiver chooses it**:
  if a caregiver adds their own free "closer look" key (see
  "A choice you control" below), a photo you ask Ilona to identify
  is sent to Google for a better answer. It is **off until a key
  is added**, and removing the key stops it completely.
- If you turn on listening, Ilona listens for her name
  ("Hey Ilona") using the microphone. Hearing her name and turning
  your words into text happen **on your phone**. Your voice is
  **never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere** — see "Ilona's voice
  and listening" below.

## What Ilona stores on your phone

Ilona stores these things **only on your phone**, so it can help you:

- Your name, and the name of your caregiver or helper, if you enter
  them during setup.
- Your medicine list: medicine names, the times you take them, and
  whether you said you took them.
- Your daily check-ins (how you slept, how you feel) and the answers
  you give.
- A diary of the help Ilona gave you — what you asked about and what
  happened. This helps you and your caregiver see patterns.
- Safety notes, such as a missed medicine reminder.
- Photos you take with "Show Ilona" (the feature that reads letters,
  labels, and signs out loud). These photos stay in the app's private
  storage on your phone.
- Copies of your information called "snapshots" (backups). Snapshots
  are **encrypted** — locked with a key — and the app keeps the last 3.
- Rough (approximate) location, **only** when you use the "Where am
  I?" feature. Ilona does not track where you go.

If you do not enter something, Ilona does not have it.

## What Ilona does NOT do

- No user accounts and no sign-in.
- No advertising and no advertising trackers.
- No selling or renting of information.
- No saving or sending of your voice. If you turn listening on,
  the microphone is used to hear "Hey Ilona" and your words, on
  the phone itself. Ilona does **not** keep recordings of your
  voice and does **not** send your voice anywhere. If listening is
  off, the microphone is not used at all.
- No face recognition. Ilona never identifies a person from a photo of
  their face.

## The one exception: Google ML Kit

Ilona uses a Google tool called **ML Kit** to read text in photos and
to name objects. The reading happens **on your phone**. Your photos
and the words in them are **not** sent to Google.

However, ML Kit itself sends Google **technical measurements** about
its own performance — for example, how often it was used and whether
it worked. This is called "API performance and utilization metrics."
It does not include your photos, your text, or your personal
information. Google explains its own practices in the Google Privacy
Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The ML Kit Terms of Service require app makers to tell you about
this. That is why this section is here.

## Ilona's voice and listening

From version 0.4.0, Ilona can talk and listen:

- **Her voice works on your phone.** Ilona speaks using voice
  software that runs on the phone itself. Nothing about her
  speaking leaves the phone.
- **Listening is your choice, and it is on the phone too.** If you
  turn on listening in Settings, Ilona listens for "Hey Ilona"
  and turns what you say into words — all on the phone. While
  listening is on, the microphone hears the room the way a person
  standing there would, but Ilona does **not** save the sound and
  does **not** send it anywhere. Sounds pass through her hearing
  and are gone; only the words she understands are kept, in the
  same help diary described above — exactly as if you had typed
  them. You can turn listening off at any time in Settings, and
  the microphone stops.
- **Her voice arrives as a download.** Her speaking voice and her
  understanding are large files that download once, after install,
  from the public software libraries that host them (GitHub and
  Hugging Face). Like any download, those services can see that a
  download happened and the internet address it went to — the same
  as downloading any app or file. The download contains no
  information about you, and nothing is uploaded.
- **If a closer-look key has been added** (see the next section),
  the words of a question you ask out loud may be sent to Google
  together with a photo, the same as a question you type. Without
  a key, your words never leave the phone.

## A choice you control: the "closer look" (Show Ilona)

Show Ilona can understand a photo in two ways:

- **On your phone (always available).** Reading words in a photo
  and naming common things happens on the phone itself. Nothing is
  sent anywhere. This is how Ilona works unless a caregiver turns
  on the closer look.
- **A closer look online (only if a caregiver adds a key).** A
  caregiver can add their own free Google Gemini key in Settings
  (under "Family & help setup"). From then on, when you ask Ilona
  to look at a photo, **that photo and your question are sent to
  Google**, which answers under the caregiver's own key and
  Google's terms (including the Google Privacy Policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy). The photo is shrunk first,
  so less is sent. If no key is added, nothing is ever sent.
  Removing the key stops it completely.

Two honest limits:

- Some photos are **never** sent, key or no key: a photo of a
  loose pill, a photo of your body, and a photo of a person.
  Those questions are always answered on the phone, by Ilona's
  safety rules.
- Google's answer appears on your screen, and the help diary notes
  that Show Ilona helped — it does not store the photo online. What
  Google does with what it receives is governed by Google's own
  policies and the caregiver's key account.

## Your caregiver and the access modes

Ilona can be set up in three ways: just for you ("Just me"), shared
with a caregiver ("Shared"), or managed by a caregiver ("Managed").

- In every mode, the caregiver sees Ilona's information **on this
  phone** — the check-ins, the medicine confirmations, the help
  diary, and the safety notes.
- Ilona does not watch your phone for anyone unless a caregiver you
  know sets up watching, in the way described in the next section.
- If you use a mode with a caregiver, choose someone you trust, the
  same way you would trust someone who helps you in your home.

## Watching from another phone (only if a caregiver sets it up)

If Ilona is set up as "Shared" or "Managed," the person helping you
can choose to see Ilona's dashboard from their own phone — the
morning check-in, the medicine confirmations and misses, the safety
notes, and the help diary. This only works if that caregiver sets
it up, in person, with your phone and theirs together.

- What travels: only that dashboard information. It is scrambled
  (encrypted) on your phone before it leaves, and it can only be
  unscrambled by your caregiver's phone. It passes through a free
  Google Firebase project that belongs to the caregiver — Google
  stores the scrambled information and cannot read it.
- What never travels: your conversations with Ilona, your messages,
  your photos, your calls, and where you are — except that a safety
  alert (for example, if you say you are lost) may include the place
  where the alert happened, inside the scrambled alert, for your
  caregiver only.
- In "Just me" mode, none of this exists. Nothing leaves your phone.
- Your caregiver can stop the watching from their phone, and the
  setup can be removed from yours. Removing it stops any new
  information from traveling; the scrambled copies already stored
  in the caregiver's project are deleted by the caregiver's app on
  removal, and in any case cannot be read by anyone else.

## Calls through Ilona (only with the watching setup)

If watching is set up, your caregiver can ask Ilona to open a call
with you, and Ilona can open a call itself after a safety alert
(for example, if you pressed for help and did not answer after).

- Ilona **always announces a call out loud first** — who is
  calling, and that they will be able to see and hear you. A call
  is never a surprise. For a call your caregiver asks for, nothing
  opens unless you answer it yourself. After a safety alert, Ilona
  announces the call more than once and waits a short while before
  opening it, so you can always say no.
- You can say no, turn the camera off during a call, or end the
  call at any time. Ending it is immediate, and the dashboard
  record says honestly that you ended it.
- Calls are **live only**. Nothing about a call is recorded: no
  sound, no picture, no written copy of what was said. The only
  thing stored is a short note that a call happened — when, what
  kind, how long, and how it ended — scrambled like the rest of
  the dashboard information.
- If a call after a safety alert cannot get through as a video
  call, Ilona says so out loud and may place an ordinary phone
  call to your caregiver's number instead, with the speaker on.
  That is a normal phone call and shows in the phone's call list.

## Keeping, deleting, and backups

- Your information stays on your phone until **you** delete it.
- You can delete diary entries, photos, and snapshots inside the app.
- Removing (uninstalling) Ilona deletes everything the app stored.
- Snapshots are kept inside the app's storage and are encrypted.
  There is a setup choice to keep one written copy of your Google
  account password on paper, held by your caregiver. That is your
  family's choice, outside the app — the app never asks for or stores
  that password.
- Ilona's automatic update checker is currently turned off because no
  update address has been set. When it is set in the future, the app
  will only look for new versions of itself; it will not send your
  information.

## Children

Ilona is made for adults. It is **not intended for children under 13**,
and it is not designed to collect information from children.

## Safety of your information

Your stored information is protected by your phone's own security and
by Android's app encryption, which uses keys kept in the phone's
secure storage.

Please understand the honest limit: **anyone who can unlock your
phone can open Ilona and see what is in it.** Keep your phone locked
with a PIN or fingerprint, and only share that with people you trust.

## "Do Not Track"

Ilona does not track you across apps or websites, so it does not
respond to "Do Not Track" signals — there is nothing to turn off.
The only outside data flows are the Google ML Kit measurement
described above and, only if a caregiver adds a key, the photos
you ask Ilona to identify (see "A choice you control").

## Your California rights

California law gives residents rights to know what personal
information a business holds about them and to ask for it to be
deleted. Ilona holds your information only on your own phone, and the
maker receives none of it. Even so: if you email the address at the
top of this page with a privacy request, it will be honored as fully
as possible, free of charge.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same
web address, the date at the top will change, and the app will show a
short note about the change. Changes will never quietly take away
the promises in "The short version."
